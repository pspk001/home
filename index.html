How to explore

  • Rotate drag · Zoom scroll / pinch · Pan right-drag / two fingers
  • Click a room (or its label / the minimap) to fly into it
  • Wall cut slices the walls so you can see inside from any angle
  • Walk mode: drag to look, WASD / arrows to move, double-click the floor to go there, climb the stairs
  • Keys: 1–4 views · L labels · F furniture · R rotate · Esc back

Ground floor modelled from the supplied plan. The first floor is shown as an outer shell matching the elevation, as its layout was not provided.
